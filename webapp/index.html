Benchmark summary

coefficient of determination on the held-out test split, mean ± s.d. over five training seeds

Arm 1 — low-fidelity numerical model, no learning. Arm 2 — data-driven surrogate mapping load to response. Arm 3 — multi-fidelity corrector: Arm 1 plus a learned residual. Reference — full non-linear finite-element solution.

Artefacts served from release_3arm_table/; databases, checkpoints, scripts and per-seed logs are archived there with an MD5 manifest. SDOF and the tunnel run live from the five archived seeds; the tunnel row is the N = 500 five-seed set the published table quotes, on the unweighted per-sensor metric. The turbine row is trained on a restricted operating envelope — the 1248 samples with hub wind speed ≤ 20.2 m/s — and its artefacts ship with the app in release_webapp/04_turbine/ rather than in the release bundle, which holds the published runs. Above rated wind the pitch controller sheds thrust and aerodynamic damping rises, so the fore-aft fluctuation shrinks against the mean deflection; excluding that regime raises the Arm 3 per-sample median from 0.68 to 0.82. It also helps Arms 1 and 2 more than Arm 3, so the multi-fidelity margin narrows — the full-range five-seed and 200%-data turbine runs remain in RESULTS.csv and data200/. The frame row archives per-seed JSON metrics only, so it replays the all-sample predictions exported from its published seed-42 run and carries no seed spread here.
